      title: An open glaciology textbook
      text: |
        <div class="opp-body">
          <p>We maintain a free, open-source textbook that introduces the physics of glaciers and ice sheets and shows how to turn that physics into working numerical models with <a href="https://icepack.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">icepack</a>. It is written as a course. It begins with the molecular physics of ice &mdash; the water molecule, the crystal, its vibrations and defects &mdash; and builds up through deformation and flow, the bed and basal processes, and the methods used to observe ice, to running a glacier model, before turning to mass balance, firn, and the climate of the past.</p>

          <p>The book is built around the distinction between the diagnostic problem (finding the velocity of an ice sheet whose shape is known) and the prognostic problem (evolving that shape in time). Many chapters carry runnable lab notebooks in a ready-to-go Docker environment, so readers can work through the computational side themselves. It is a living document, and corrections and contributions are welcome.</p>

          <p><strong><a href="https://rice-glaciology.github.io/glaciology-book/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Read the textbook &rarr;</a></strong></p>
