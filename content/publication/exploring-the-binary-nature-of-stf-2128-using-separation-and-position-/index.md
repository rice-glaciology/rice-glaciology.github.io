abstract: A team of nine students from Cuesta College studied double star STF 2128
  (WDS 17033+ 5935) using ten CCD images obtained at the Sierra Remote Observatories.
  Calculations of these ten observations yielded an average separation of 12.203 ″and
  an average position angle of 42.957. By comparing these values with past observations
  from the Washington Double Star Catalogue, we concluded that STF 2128 is likely
  a true binary system.
