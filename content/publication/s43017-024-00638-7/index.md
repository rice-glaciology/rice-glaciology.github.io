abstract: Antarctic atmospheric rivers (ARs) are a form of extreme weather that transport
  heat and moisture from the Southern Hemisphere subtropics to the Antarctic continent.
  AR events generally have a positive influence on the Antarctic ice-sheet mass balance
  by producing heavy snowfall, yet they also cause melt of sea ice and coastal ice
  sheet areas, as well as ice shelf destabilization.
